# English For Today (Classes XI-XII & Alim) — Interactive E-Book & Vocab Studio

An interactive, responsive digital e-book platform designed for **NCTB English For Today (Classes XI-XII & Alim)**, featuring Magoosh-style vocabulary learning, bilingual Bengali dictionary, precision textbook word marking, and multiple reading view modes.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **📖 3 Dynamic Reading Modes**:
  - **Formatted Text Mode**: Clean typography, high-contrast readable format, audio narration.
  - **Split View Mode**: Side-by-side comparison of original textbook scans with editable, selectable formatted text.
  - **Original Scanned Page Mode**: High-resolution textbook pages.

- **🎓 Magoosh-Style Vocabulary Builder & MCQ Practice Platform**:
  - Full coverage across **12 Units and 47 Lessons**.
  - Interactive MCQs with instant feedback, Bangla meanings, and textbook contexts.
  - **`📖 View in Textbook →`**: Redirects directly to the exact page and highlights the word in the textbook with a glowing golden mark and centered scroll.

- **🔤 Grammatical Forms & Morphological Engine**:
  - **4 Parts of Speech**: Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb.
  - **Verb Tenses & Conjugation**: Present (V1), Past (V2), Past Participle (V3), Continuous (V4), and Future.
  - **Degrees of Comparison**: Positive, Comparative, Superlative.
  - **Synonyms & Antonyms**: Side-by-side pill cards for vocabulary expansion.

- **🔊 Audio Synthesis & Smart TTS**:
  - In-browser natural voice narration with variable speed controls (0.75x to 1.5x).
  - Sound effects for page flips, quiz success chimes, and word focus.

- **📝 Study Tools**:
  - Multi-color highlighter (Yellow, Green, Pink).
  - Personal study notes and page bookmarks saved in local storage.
  - Full-text search across all 295 pages.
